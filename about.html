About

“I like solving complex problems, and building products that spark joy: the kind people actually use and notice.”

I started in a design room building CAD and FEA models for truck load bodies, and in a service bay pulling apart engines and gearboxes that had failed in the field. That taught me something I still use: a vehicle is one system, and most of the trouble lives in the seams between its parts.

Most engineering problems I've worked on since weren't hard because the physics was hard. They were hard because the requirement, the model, the test and the thing that shipped had quietly drifted apart, and nobody noticed until it was expensive. I've spent over a decade closing that gap: first in controls, taking algorithms from a clean sheet through production, then in systems engineering, connecting requirements, architecture and simulation. My experience across engineering centers in the US, Japan, South Korea, Europe and India has taught me to look for the real pain point behind a request, then build a structured way to solve it.

Now I'm exploring agentic AI workflows in systems engineering, where the agent works from formal models and its output is checked before anyone relies on it.

What I work with
  • Requirements engineering and traceability
  • System architecture and model-based systems engineering
  • Safety analysis: FHA, FMEA and FTA
  • Model-based design, with MIL, SIL and HIL verification
  • ASPICE-aligned process and agile delivery in safety-critical programs
